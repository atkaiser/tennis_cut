Can a silent pro swing yield one trustworthy contact timestamp?

This demo asks whether the existing single-frame shot classifier can be scanned as a contact locator, or whether Choose visual contact localization for silent pro swings should specify a dedicated temporal contact model and explicit fatal-ambiguity states.

Evidence boundary: these traces come from five labeled swings in the repository’s user-video fixture, evaluated without audio. They test the existing model’s score shape; they do not establish accuracy on professional footage.

    Scan the existing classifier

    Reject. It recognizes a broad swing-like pose. Four peak timestamps happen to land near contact, but one is 272 ms early and no trace has a unique peak.

    Dedicated temporal contact model

    Recommend. Score each candidate frame from a short before/after sequence and train specifically against human-labeled contact frames.

    Racket/ball geometry

    Do not require for the first approach. It adds two difficult detectors and fails when a tiny ball is blurred or occluded; revisit only if the temporal model misses its accuracy contract.

    Proposed interface: one module accepts an eligible pro video and returns either one contact timestamp with calibrated evidence or a fatal result: low confidence, competing peaks, boundary peak, or ineligible footage. Exact thresholds belong to the downstream accuracy-contract decision.
