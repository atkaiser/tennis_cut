# Use visual evidence for user contact selection

User contact frames and their confidence are selected from visual ball-and-racket evidence rather than acoustic peak timing. Sound propagation and recording synchronization can displace an audio event from the corresponding video image, while comparison requires the closest available visual representation of contact. Audio locates broad candidate regions, but only visual evidence from a frame and its neighbors scores the discrete source frames. The detector does not invent an interpolated contact timestamp: the strongest existing frame is selected, ties resolve toward the earlier frame, and a region without sufficiently strong evidence is omitted.

This refinement belongs to `tennis-compare`; the legacy `tennis-cut` workflow retains its audio-derived timing. During staged shot-type rollout, comparison requests for an unsupported pro shot type fail clearly rather than falling back to audio alignment.

The accepted forehand prototype uses stock YOLO detections as temporal features rather than treating per-frame detection confidence as contact confidence. Selection focuses on the largest person, rejects rackets associated with other people and stationary ball detections, and considers direct racket-ball proximity, short contact-time ball disappearance, and ball-direction change. A deterministic scorer selects the discrete source frame; a lightweight temporal ranker corroborates the selection and supplies contact confidence. A disagreement wider than one frame is omitted.

On the 101-forehand compact pilot, with adjacent camera-roll families held out from their predictions, the operating threshold included 49 swings: 47 landed on the weak manual label or one adjacent frame (95.9%), and 25 landed on the manual frame (51.0%). The confirmed pro probe selected frame 635 exactly. This evidence supports implementing the visual selector and temporal confidence gate before considering detector fine-tuning; selection and confidence remain replaceable so later failure analysis can change either independently.
